Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026

Your drafts stay in your browser

The workspace stores your project in your browser’s local storage so it can reopen on this device. We do not receive, sync, or inspect that content. Clearing site data removes the draft; export JSON first if you want a backup.

Speech stays on device

Speech playback uses your browser and operating system’s speech synthesis capability. This product does not send description text to a hosted speech or AI service. Your browser vendor’s implementation is governed by its own settings and policies.

No accounts or behavioral tracking

There are no accounts, advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party runtime scripts. The hosting platform may retain short-lived security and request logs as needed to operate the site.

Exports are yours

Imported files are read locally. Exported JSON is downloaded directly by your browser. You decide where to store or share it.

Questions

Open an issue in the public repository for product or privacy questions. Do not include private project content in a public issue.

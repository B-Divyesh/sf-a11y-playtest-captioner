Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026

Your drafts stay in your browser

The workspace stores your project in browser local storage so it can reopen on this device. It does not send project content to a product server. Clearing site data removes the draft; export JSON first if you want a backup.

Speech uses your browser

Speech playback uses your browser and operating system’s speech feature. This product does not send caption text to a hosted speech or AI service. Your browser vendor’s implementation follows its own settings and policies.

No product tracking

The demo has no sign-in form. It sets no product cookies and loads no third-party runtime scripts. The hosting platform may retain short-lived security and request logs as needed to operate the site.

Exports are yours

Imported files are read locally. Exported JSON is downloaded directly by your browser. You decide where to store or share it.

Questions

Open an issue in the public repository (external) for product or privacy questions. Do not include private project content in a public issue.
